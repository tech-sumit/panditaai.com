THE PANDIT.AI NEXUS

Kaushal was conceptualized as the hardware-integrated extension of the Pandit.ai framework. While Pandit provides the cognitive reasoning, Kaushal provides the physical skill execution.

Our origin story begins with a simple question: "Why can AI reason about physics but fail to manipulate a screwdriver?" We solved the translation layer between high-level logic and low-level torque.
